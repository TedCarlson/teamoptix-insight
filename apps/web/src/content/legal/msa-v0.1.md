# TeamOptix Insight Master Service Agreement

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Internal Working Draft  
**Product:** Insight  
**Provider:** TeamOptix  
**Last Updated:** 2026-06-28  

> This document is an internal working draft prepared for business planning and future legal review. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before being used as a binding customer agreement.

## 1. Purpose

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") establishes the general terms under which TeamOptix provides access to the Insight platform to customer organizations.

Insight is an operations management platform designed to help organizations reduce administrative burden, improve operational visibility, organize workforce and company data, support reporting workflows, and make better operational decisions.

This Agreement may be supplemented by order forms, subscription schedules, service policies, privacy policies, data processing terms, support policies, or other written addenda approved by TeamOptix.

## 2. Parties

This Agreement is between TeamOptix ("Provider," "we," "us," or "our") and the customer organization that subscribes to or uses Insight ("Customer," "you," or "your").

Each party is responsible for ensuring that the person accepting or signing this Agreement has authority to bind that party.

## 3. Definitions

"Insight" means the software platform, applications, workflows, interfaces, reports, automation tools, dashboards, documentation, and related services provided by TeamOptix.

"Customer Data" means data, files, records, reports, user information, operational information, business information, and other content submitted to or processed through Insight by or on behalf of Customer.

"Authorized Users" means employees, contractors, representatives, or other individuals authorized by Customer to access Insight.

"Subscription" means the commercial package, plan, tier, or service level purchased or activated for Customer.

"Order Form" means a written or electronic ordering document, subscription schedule, invoice, or other commercial record accepted by TeamOptix and Customer.

## 4. Scope of Services

TeamOptix will provide Customer access to Insight according to the applicable subscription, order form, and platform configuration.

Insight may include features for workforce management, company operations, dispatch support, asset tracking, payroll support, report ingestion, automation, analytics, intelligence, and other operational workflows.

TeamOptix may modify, improve, or discontinue features from time to time, provided that such changes do not materially reduce the core functionality of an active paid subscription without reasonable notice.

## 5. Customer Responsibilities

Customer is responsible for:

- maintaining accurate company, user, workforce, operational, and billing information;
- ensuring Authorized Users use Insight in accordance with this Agreement;
- obtaining any permissions required to submit Customer Data to Insight;
- reviewing reports, outputs, recommendations, or automated results before relying on them for business decisions;
- maintaining appropriate internal controls over its business operations;
- promptly notifying TeamOptix of suspected unauthorized access or security concerns.

Customer remains responsible for decisions made using information produced or organized by Insight.

## 6. Subscription, Billing, and Payment

Customer agrees to pay all fees associated with its subscription, order form, or approved commercial agreement.

Insight determines the commercial relationship, subscription status, billing schedule, invoice records, and applicable billing policies. Payment processing may be performed by a third-party payment provider.

TeamOptix may offer trial periods, promotional pricing, custom pricing, grace periods, or manual billing arrangements at its discretion.

Unless otherwise stated in an order form, fees are due according to the billing cadence and payment terms provided by TeamOptix.

## 7. Customer Data Ownership

Customer retains ownership of Customer Data.

TeamOptix may process Customer Data only as needed to provide, secure, maintain, improve, support, and operate Insight; comply with legal obligations; prevent misuse; and enforce this Agreement.

Customer grants TeamOptix a limited right to host, process, transmit, display, organize, analyze, and use Customer Data for the purpose of providing Insight and related services.

## 8. Data Accuracy and Operational Outputs

Insight may collect, ingest, transform, summarize, or analyze information from customer uploads, third-party systems, automation workflows, or manual entries.

TeamOptix will use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve data integrity and provide useful operational outputs.

Customer understands that operational reports, analytics, automation results, and intelligence outputs may depend on the quality, timeliness, and completeness of source data. Customer is responsible for reviewing outputs before making employment, payroll, financial, compliance, operational, or customer-impacting decisions.

## 9. Security and Privacy

TeamOptix will use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect Customer Data.

TeamOptix does not intend to store raw payment card information inside Insight. Payment information should be handled by third-party payment processors where applicable.

Privacy, data processing, retention, deletion, and security details may be further described in separate policies or addenda.

## 10. Acceptable Use

Customer and Authorized Users may not use Insight to:

- violate applicable laws or regulations;
- upload malicious code or interfere with platform operation;
- attempt unauthorized access to systems or data;
- misuse automation or platform features;
- submit data Customer does not have authority to use;
- reverse engineer, copy, resell, or exploit Insight except as expressly permitted;
- use Insight in a way that harms TeamOptix, other customers, or platform integrity.

## 11. Intellectual Property

TeamOptix owns Insight, including software, workflows, platform architecture, designs, documentation, templates, proprietary methods, product concepts, improvements, and related intellectual property.

Customer owns Customer Data.

No ownership rights are transferred except as expressly stated in this Agreement.

Feedback, suggestions, or improvement ideas provided to TeamOptix may be used by TeamOptix without restriction or compensation, provided that TeamOptix does not disclose Customer confidential information.

## 12. Confidentiality

Each party may receive confidential business, technical, operational, financial, or customer information from the other party.

The receiving party will use reasonable care to protect confidential information and will not use or disclose it except as needed to perform under this Agreement or as required by law.

Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that is publicly available, already known without restriction, independently developed, or lawfully received from another source.

## 13. Service Availability and Support

TeamOptix will use commercially reasonable efforts to make Insight available and support the platform.

Insight may be unavailable during maintenance, upgrades, security events, outages, third-party service interruptions, or circumstances outside TeamOptix's reasonable control.

Support expectations, maintenance windows, response targets, and service policies may be described in a separate support policy or order form.

## 14. Third-Party Services

Insight may integrate with or rely on third-party services, including hosting providers, authentication providers, storage providers, automation tools, email providers, analytics providers, payment processors, or customer-designated systems.

TeamOptix is not responsible for third-party service failures outside its reasonable control, but will use reasonable efforts to maintain platform continuity where practical.

## 15. Warranties and Disclaimers

TeamOptix will provide Insight in a professional and commercially reasonable manner.

Except as expressly stated, Insight is provided "as is" and "as available." TeamOptix does not warrant that Insight will be uninterrupted, error-free, or that all outputs will be complete, accurate, or fit for every customer purpose.

Customer is responsible for evaluating whether Insight meets its business, operational, compliance, and reporting needs.

## 16. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, TeamOptix will not be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, punitive, exemplary, or lost-profit damages arising from or related to Insight.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, TeamOptix's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid by Customer to TeamOptix for Insight during the three months preceding the event giving rise to the claim, unless a different limit is stated in an applicable order form.

## 17. Indemnification

Customer will defend and indemnify TeamOptix against claims arising from Customer Data, Customer's misuse of Insight, violation of this Agreement, violation of law, or infringement caused by materials Customer provides.

TeamOptix will defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims alleging that Insight, as provided by TeamOptix, infringes intellectual property rights, subject to reasonable exclusions and limitations to be finalized during legal review.

## 18. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins when Customer accepts the Agreement, signs an order form, starts a subscription, or otherwise accesses Insight under an approved TeamOptix arrangement.

Either party may terminate according to the applicable order form, subscription terms, billing policy, or written agreement.

TeamOptix may suspend or terminate access for nonpayment, security risk, unlawful use, material breach, or activity that threatens platform integrity.

## 19. Effect of Termination

Upon termination, Customer's access to Insight may end or be limited.

TeamOptix may provide a reasonable opportunity for Customer to export available Customer Data, subject to account status, technical feasibility, payment status, legal obligations, and applicable retention policies.

TeamOptix may retain or delete Customer Data according to its data retention policy, legal obligations, backup practices, and platform operations.

## 20. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

The governing law, venue, and dispute resolution process should be finalized with legal counsel before customer use.

## 21. General Provisions

This Agreement, together with applicable order forms, policies, and addenda, represents the agreement between TeamOptix and Customer regarding Insight.

If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain effective.

Customer may not assign this Agreement without TeamOptix's prior written consent, except as may be allowed in a future legally reviewed version.

TeamOptix may update this Agreement from time to time. Material updates should be versioned and communicated according to the applicable document governance policy.

## 22. Document Governance

This Agreement is version controlled.

Each active customer agreement should reference the applicable Agreement version, effective date, and acceptance record.
